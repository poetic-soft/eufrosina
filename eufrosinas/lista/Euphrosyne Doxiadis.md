# Euphrosyne Doxiadis

**Euphrosyne Doxiadis** (born 19 April 1946) is a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks "Greeks") artist, art historian, and writer.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrosyne_Doxiadis#cite_note-1) In 2025, she authored *NG6461: The Fake Rubens* and outlined her belief that [Sir Peter Paul Rubens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Paul_Rubens "Peter Paul Rubens")' [*Samson and Delilah*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samson_and_Delilah_(Rubens) "Samson and Delilah (Rubens)") (1609–1610) is an [art forgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_forgery "Art forgery").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrosyne_Doxiadis#cite_note-NG6461-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrosyne_Doxiadis#cite_note-3)
