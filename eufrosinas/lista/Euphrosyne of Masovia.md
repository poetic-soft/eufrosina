# Euphrosyne of Masovia

**Euphrosyne of Masovia** also known as *Eufrozja* (1292–1328/1329) was Duchess of [Oświęcim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O%C5%9Bwi%C4%99cim "Oświęcim") by her marriage. She served in the regency of her son John, in the duchy of Oświęcim.
